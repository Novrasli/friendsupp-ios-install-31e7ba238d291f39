FriendSupp

iOS 1.0.25 · Build 26 · Firebase Beta

Private Ad Hoc prototype for registered iPhones only. No real payments.

Update FriendSupp Open in Safari on your registered second iPhone and tap Update FriendSupp. iOS installs new signed versions over the existing app — do not delete FriendSupp first. Confirm the installation prompt if shown. Your locally stored app data is intended to stay in place, although this demo prototype does not currently persist all session activity. Only registered iPhones can install the IPA.
